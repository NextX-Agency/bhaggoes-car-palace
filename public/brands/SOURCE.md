The SVGs in this directory come from [Sim1Who/CarBrands](https://github.com/Sim1Who/CarBrands), an MIT-licensed icon collection designed by Dan G. Nelson. The source repository's license and authorship notice are retained here. Car brand names and marks remain the property of their respective owners and are displayed only to identify listed vehicles.

`isuzu-wordmark.svg` is the Isuzu wordmark from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Isuzu_wordmark.svg), credited there to Isuzu and marked public domain as a simple text logo.

Files prefixed `vs-` are selected brand marks from [VehicleSpecs brand-logos](https://github.com/vehiclespecs/brand-logos), mapped in `src/lib/vehicle-brand-assets.json`. Its metadata is MIT licensed; the logos themselves remain the trademarks of their respective owners. They are used here only to identify vehicle makes. The matching import script is `scripts/import-brand-logos.mjs`.

Files prefixed `commons-` come from Wikimedia Commons. Individual source links are recorded in `COMMONS-SOURCE.md`; each linked page gives its license details.

`kenworth-official.png` is from [Kenworth's website](https://www.kenworth.com/media/w4jnzm4t/kenworth_logo-header-new-012023.png). `gac-official.webp` is from [GAC's website](https://www.gacgroup.com/en). Both are used only to identify their vehicles.

`ashok-leyland-logo.png` and `bestune-logo.png` are from [CarBrandLogos](https://carbrandlogos.com/), and `mack-logo.png` is from [CarBrandNames](https://carbrandnames.com/brands/mack/). These brand marks are used only for vehicle identification.
